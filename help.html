1. Getting Started

First Time on a New Device

When you open the app for the first time on any device, you'll see the Data Sync Setup screen. You need a team code from your admin to connect.

1
Open the application URL in your browser (Chrome, Edge, or Firefox recommended).
2
Click "I Have a Team Code" and paste the code your admin provided.
3
Once connected, click "Go to Login" to proceed to the login page.
💡 Note: You only need to enter the team code once per device/browser. After setup, data syncs automatically.

Requesting an Account

If you don't have an account yet:

1
On the login page, click "Request Account".
2
Enter your first name, last name, USPS email, and choose a password (minimum 4 characters).
3
Click "Submit Request". Your request will be sent to an admin for approval.
4
Once approved, you can log in with your USPS email and the password you chose.
✅ Tip: The system will try to match your name and email to an existing team member in the database. If matched, your account will be linked to your existing assignments.

Logging In

1
On the login page, click "Log In".
2
Enter your USPS email (just the part before @usps.gov) and your password.
3
After logging in, you'll be taken to the Home page where you'll see your assigned reviews.
💡 Note: If you forget your password, contact your Review Lead or Admin to reset it.

2. Data Sync Setup

All data is automatically synced across devices through a shared cloud store. This means you can use the app on your phone, laptop, or any browser and see the same data.

How It Works

  • Data saves to your browser instantly (works offline too).
  • Changes sync to the cloud within a few seconds when online.
  • Other devices pick up changes when they refresh the page or within 10 seconds automatically.

Getting the Team Code

The team code is provided by your admin. It connects your device to the shared data store. You only need to enter it once per device.

⚠️ Important: Do not share the team code outside your review team. It provides access to all shared data.

2. Understanding Roles

System Roles (Your Account Level)

Your system role determines what pages and features you can access across the entire application.

RoleWhat You Can Do
Admin Full access: manage users, manage all reviews, approve requests, system reset, view all data. No time restrictions.
Review Lead Create and manage review packages, set up travel, build schedules, assign teams, manage workbook. Can access Admin panel (Reviews tab only). 30-day extended access window.
POD (Reviewer) Enter observations, fill out forms, submit data for assigned reviews. Data locks 1 week after review ends.
Read Only View dashboards and summaries. Cannot edit or enter data.

Review Roles (Your Role Per Review)

Within each review, you are assigned a specific review role that determines which tabs and features you see.

Review RoleWhat You See
Clerk Reviewer Clerk Notes tab for time-based clerk observations. All standard tabs (PS 3922, Trips, SCF Hub, ADUS/SDUS, Questionnaire, Summarization, End of Day).
Mail Handler Reviewer Mail Handler Notes tab instead of Clerk Notes. Tracks a specific MH employee per observation session. Same supporting tabs as Clerk.
Workbook Lead Full workbook management with Pre-Review, Review, and Post-Review phases. Can view and edit all reviewer data. Can also enter their own observations via "My Notes" mode.
Review Lead Everything Workbook Lead has, plus Travel management, Schedule builder, Finalize checklist, and Information tabs.

3. Home Page & Review Cards

The Home page is your dashboard. Here you'll find:

Quick Actions

  • Start Review Day — Begin entering data for a review day. Takes you to the setup page.
  • View Summary — Open the Review Dashboard to see aggregate data across all reviews.
  • Create Review (Leads/Admins only) — Opens the Admin panel to create a new review.

Review Cards

Each review you're assigned to appears as a card with:

  • Review Name — The name of the review (e.g., "Erie S&DC Review")
  • Date Range — Start and end dates of the review period
  • Offices — Which USPS offices are being reviewed
  • Your Role — Your assigned role for this review (badge)
  • Progress Indicators — Day 1 and Day 2 status dots
  • Action Buttons:
    • Start Day 1 / Start Day 2 — Begin a new day of observations
    • Edit Day 1 / Day 2 — Return to a day you've already started
    • Manage Review (Leads) — Open the workbook view
    • Travel ✈️ — Travel survey status and link
💡 Review Timing: You can only start/edit reviews within the active window. PODs: up to 1 week after the review ends. Leads: up to 30 days after. After that, data is locked and you can only "View Summary."

4. POD (Reviewer) Workflow

Starting Your Review Day

1
From the Home page, find your review card and click "Start Day 1".
2
You'll be taken to the Review page. Your Observation Info bar at the top shows the office, date, day number, and your role.
3
Your workflow is divided into 3 phases (buttons shown below the Day toggle):
  • Pre-Review — Review schedules and complete travel survey (if applicable)
  • Review — Enter all observation data across tabs
  • Post-Review — Confirm expense report submission

Day Toggle

Use the Day 1 / Day 2 buttons to switch between observation days. Each day has its own separate data.

Phase Workflow

Pre-Review Phase

  • View the Schedules tab to see when you need to arrive and where.
  • Complete the Travel Survey if your lead has assigned one (flight/driving details, hotel booking).

Review Phase (Main Data Entry)

This is where you'll spend most of your time. Switch between the tabs along the top to enter data:

  • Clerk Notes or MH Notes — Your primary observation entries
  • PS 3922 — Volume recording by zone
  • Record Trips — Truck arrival container counts
  • SCF Hub — Tally counts for SCF operations
  • ADUS/SDUS — Machine throughput runs
  • Questionnaire — Y/N questions about observed operations
  • Summarization — Summary comments linked to specific tabs
  • End of Day — Run validation checks and submit your day
✅ Autosave: All your data is automatically saved as you type. You'll see a save indicator. You can close your browser and come back — your data will still be there.

Post-Review Phase

  • Confirm that you have submitted your expense report by checking the box and clicking "Confirm".

5. Clerk Notes Tab

This is your primary observation entry tab if you are a Clerk Reviewer.

How to Enter Observations

1
Each row represents one observation period. Fields include:
  • Begin Time / End Time — The time window of the observation (HH:MM format)
  • LDC — Labor Distribution Code (dropdown: LDC 41, 42, 43A, 43L, 43F, 43P, 44, 45, 48, NP, Break, Lunch, Comfort Break, Cross Craft)
  • OPN — Operation Number (auto-populates based on selected LDC)
  • Total Clerks — Number of clerks observed in this operation
  • Work Quality — Assessment of work quality
  • Description — Free-text notes about the observation
2
Click "+ Add Row" at the bottom to add a new observation entry.
3
Data is saved automatically. The elapsed time for each entry is calculated from Begin → End time × Total Clerks.
💡 LDC Reference:
41 = ADUS/SDUS  |  42 = BRM/PD/PRS  |  43A = Allied Duties  |  43L = Manual Flats
43F = Manual Letters  |  43P = Manual Parcels  |  44 = PO Box Distribution  |  45 = Retail Window
48 = Misc Customer Service  |  NP = Non-Productive  |  BRK = Break  |  LUN = Lunch

7. PS Form 3922 Tab

Record mail volume data by zone for the PS Form 3922.

How to Use

  1. Click "+ Add Zone" to create a zone tab (e.g., "ALL ZONES", "Zone 1-3").
  2. Enter volume counts in the worksheet grid for each zone.
  3. Use the PO Box DPS section for DPS piece counts.
  4. Piece Conversion auto-calculates:
    • Letters: 221 per 12-inch tray
    • Flats: 115 per 12-inch tray
  5. Switch between zones using the tabs at the top of the section.

8. Record Trips Tab

Track truck arrivals and container counts throughout the day.

How to Use

  1. An "On Hand" trip is auto-created for the initial mail count.
  2. Click "+ Add Trip" when a new truck arrives.
  3. Enter container counts for each trip (letter trays, flat tubs, parcels, etc.).
  4. Use Mail Flow & Containers Notes to add free-text notes about deliveries.

9. SCF Hub Tab

Tally counts for Sectional Center Facility operations.

How to Use

  1. Use the +/− buttons to tally counts for each operation:
    • AM/PM Cross Dock, AM/PM Hash, PM Carwash
    • Unit, Registry Seals, HCR Seals
  2. Add comments/descriptions for each tally row.
  3. Use the SCF Operation Notes text area for general observations.

10. ADUS/SDUS Tab

Track automated machine throughput runs.

How to Use

  1. Click "+ Add Machine" and enter the ZIP code and machine type (SDUS+, ADUS, or SDUS).
  2. For each machine, click "+ Add Run" to log a throughput run:
    • Start/End Time — Automatically calculates duration
    • Total Pieces — Automatically calculates throughput (pieces/hour)
    • Rejects, No Reads, No Barcode — Automatically calculates percentages
  3. Switch between machines using the tabs at the top.

11. Questionnaire Tab

Answer observation questions about the operations you witnessed during your review day.

How It Works

  • The questionnaire dynamically shows only the LDCs you observed in your Clerk Notes.
  • For each question: select Yes/No, choose a result, and add comments if needed.
  • If you haven't entered any Clerk Notes yet, the questionnaire will be empty — go fill out Clerk Notes first.

12. Summarization Tab

Write summary comments that reference specific tabs and findings.

How to Use

  1. Click "+ Add Comment" to create a new summary entry.
  2. Select which source tab the comment relates to (Clerk Notes, PS 3922, Trips, etc.).
  3. Write your summary comment. These comments are compiled by the Workbook Lead.

13. Travel Survey

If your Review Lead has assigned you to the travel survey, you'll see this tab during the Pre-Review phase.

How to Complete

  1. Enter your phone number.
  2. Select your travel mode: Flying ✈️ or Driving 🚗.
  3. If flying, fill in arrival and departure flight details:
    • Airport code, date, time, airline, flight number
  4. Check "Hotel has been booked" if applicable.
  5. Click "Submit Travel Survey".
💡 Hotel Booking: If your lead provided a hotel booking link, you'll see it as a banner at the top of the Travel Survey tab.

14. Schedules Tab

View the schedules created by your Review Lead. Available during the Pre-Review phase.

What You'll See

  • Review Schedule — When to arrive at the office and which employees to observe. Reviewers arrive 10 minutes before the employee's start time.
  • Daily Trips to Office — Transportation groups (who's riding together).
  • Airport Schedule — Pickup and dropoff details for flying team members.

15. End of Day Tab

Complete your review day by running validation checks and submitting.

How to Submit Your Day

1
Click "Run Review Check" to scan all your tabs for issues (missing data, time gaps, etc.).
2
Review each flag that appears. Click on a flag to jump to the tab where the issue is.
3
Mark each flag as reviewed (dismiss it) after addressing or acknowledging the issue.
4
Once all flags are cleared, the "Submit Day" button appears. Click it to finalize your day.

Observation Timeline

Below the validation section, you'll see a visual timeline of your observation entries. Each block represents one entry, with width proportional to duration. Blocks are color-coded by LDC (clerks) or task category (mail handlers).

16. Lead / Workbook Workflow

If you are a Workbook Lead or Review Lead, you have a comprehensive management view.

Accessing Your Workbook

  1. From the Home page, click "Manage Review" on your review card.
  2. You'll enter the Workbook view with 3 phases: Pre-Review, Review, Post-Review.

Mode Toggle

At the top of the workbook, you'll see two modes:

  • 📋 Manage — View and manage all team data, schedules, and workbook tools.
  • 📝 My Notes — Switch to POD view to enter your own observations (same as what reviewers see).

Multi-Office Reviews

If your review covers multiple offices, use the office buttons at the top to switch between offices. Each office has its own data, schedule, and roster.

17. Lead: Pre-Review Phase

Prepare everything before the review begins.

Sub-Tabs Available

Information (Calendar & Documents)

  • View the review calendar with key dates and milestones.
  • Upload reference documents (PDF, Word, Excel, images) organized by category.

Office Schedule (Roster)

  1. Upload a WebCOINS export file (.xls) — the Employee/Job List for Function 4.
  2. The system parses employee names, pay levels, start times, and seniority data.
  3. Optionally upload a TACS Clock Ring CSV to compare actual vs. scheduled start times.
💡 WebCOINS Export: In WebCOINS, navigate to Employee/Job List → Function 4. Export should include: Pay Level, Non-Scheduled Days, Seniority Date, Seniority Number, and Start Times.

Schedule Builder

  • Auto-generates a review schedule from the office roster start times.
  • Reviewers arrive 10 minutes before each employee's start time.
  • Toggle between Bid Times and Actual BT (from TACS clock ring data).
  • Add reviewer rows, reset the schedule, and export.

Assign Review Team

  • Search for existing users by last name and assign them to the review.
  • Create new user accounts directly from this panel.
  • Auto-formatted names: "LAST, FIRST" with default password.

Travel (Review Lead Only)

  • Set an optional hotel booking link for the team.
  • Import travelers from the Schedule tab or add individually.
  • Track who has responded to the travel survey.
  • Build transportation groups — assign driver/Uber Pool groups.
  • Use "Auto-Group" to automatically group travelers by arrival time.

Schedule Email

  • Build and send three types of schedule emails:
    1. Review Schedule — assignment details for the team
    2. Daily Trips — transportation groups for each day
    3. Airport Schedule — flight pickup/dropoff details
  • Auto-populated To/CC fields, "📧 Open in Outlook" integration.

Finalize (Review Lead Only)

  • Run a Pre-Review Checklist that scans for incomplete setup items.
  • Click flagged items to navigate directly to the issue.

18. Lead: Review Phase

Manage and monitor the review while it's in progress.

Sub-Tabs Available

TACS Data (Clock Rings)

  • Upload a TACS CSV export to import employee clock ring data.
  • Clerks and Mail Handlers are auto-detected by D/A code matching.
  • Flags employees who are on the clock but not in the office roster.

CSAW / DUT

  • CSAW Earned Hours — grid input for LDCs 41–48 by Day 1 and Day 2.
  • DUT / POBUT — text fields for daily entry.

Questionnaire

  • Full questionnaire covering all LDCs (unlike the POD version which filters by observed LDCs).

Timelines

  • Observation Timelines — visual rollup showing all reviewers' observation timelines overlapping on a shared time axis.
  • Filter by: Craft (Clerk / Mail Handler), Day (1 / 2), Reviewer (all or individual).
  • Click "Refresh" to load/update the view.
  • Color-coded blocks with tooltips showing observation details.

Status

  • Reviewer Status Table — shows Day 1/Day 2 submission status for every assigned reviewer.
  • Status indicators: Not Started, ⏳ Draft, ✓ Submitted.
  • Error count column flags data quality issues.
  • Click any reviewer's day status or action button to open their notes for review/editing.
✅ Reviewing POD Data: In the Status tab, click a reviewer's Day 1 or Day 2 link to open their observation data. A yellow banner will show "Viewing [Name]'s notes (Lead Edit Mode)". Click "← Back to Workbook" to return.

My Notes

  • View the current office and your own observation status (Day 1 / Day 2).
  • Clerk Notes by Reviewer — click any reviewer card to preview their observations in a popup.
  • Mail Handler Notes — view MH observations by employee.

19. Lead: Post-Review Phase

Sub-Tabs Available

Expense Report

  • Summary view showing which team members have confirmed their expense reports.
  • "📧 Remind Pending" — send reminders to team members who haven't confirmed.
  • Your own confirmation checkbox + Confirm button.

Workbook Review

  • Upload Workbook — drag & drop or browse to upload the completed workbook file (.xlsx, .xlsb, .xls, .csv).
  • Review Status Pipeline:
    1. Workbook Uploaded
    2. Review Lead Reviewed
    3. Approved
  • Lead Actions: Mark as Reviewed, ✓ Approve, ↩ Send Back (with return note), ⟲ Reset Status.

20. Admin Panel

Access: Admin users see all tabs. Review Leads see the Reviews tab only.

Manage My Reviews Tab

  1. Create a Review:
    • Enter review name, start/end dates.
    • Add offices using the office picker (search by name or finance number). Each office can have its own date range.
    • Assign leadership: select a user and role (Workbook Lead or Review Lead).
    • Click "Create Review".
  2. All Reviews Table: view, edit, or delete existing reviews.
  3. Edit Review: modify any review details — name, dates, offices, and team assignments.

Manage Users Tab (Admin Only)

  • Account Requests: review and approve/deny account requests from new users. Each request shows the user's name, email, and whether they match an existing team member. Choose a role (POD, Review Lead, Read Only, Admin) before approving. Click ↻ Refresh to pull the latest requests without reloading the page.
  • Bulk Create Users: paste a list of names (one per line), select a role, and click "Add Users".
  • All Users Table: view all users with their role, assigned offices, and status. Edit or delete any user.
  • Edit User: change display name, email, password, role, or assigned offices.
  • "Reset to Default Password": resets a user's password so they must set a new one on next login.

System Tab

  • Go Live — Reset for Production: Keeps all user accounts but resets passwords to defaults and deletes all review/observation data. Type "GO LIVE" to confirm.
  • Full Factory Reset: Deletes everything including user accounts. Type "DELETE" to confirm.
⚠️ Warning: Both reset actions are permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure to export any needed data before resetting.

22. FAQ & Troubleshooting

General

Q: I can't see my review on the Home page.

Your Review Lead needs to assign you to the review. Contact them to verify your assignment. Also check that the review dates haven't expired.

Q: My data disappeared!

Data is stored locally in your browser. If you cleared your browser data, history, or cache, the data may have been deleted. Avoid clearing browser data during an active review. Always use the same browser/computer.

Q: I can't edit my review — it says "View Summary" only.

The review has expired. POD data locks 1 week after the review end date. Contact your admin if you need an extension.

Q: I don't see all the tabs.

Tabs are phase-dependent. Make sure you're in the correct phase (Pre-Review, Review, or Post-Review). Some tabs only appear during the Review phase.

Data Entry

Q: My time entries show errors in End of Day.

Check for: missing Begin/End times, time gaps between entries, missing LDC codes, and missing work quality assessments. Click the flag in End of Day to jump to the issue.

Q: How do I switch between Day 1 and Day 2?

Use the Day 1 / Day 2 toggle buttons below the Observation Info bar. Each day has completely separate data.

Q: Can I go back and edit previous entries?

Yes, as long as the review window is still open. Just click "Edit Day 1" or "Edit Day 2" from the Home page.

Leads

Q: How do I view a reviewer's data?

Go to the Status sub-tab in your workbook's Review phase. Click any reviewer's day link or the D1/D2 action buttons to open their notes in edit mode.

Q: How do I get back to my workbook after viewing a reviewer's data?

Click the "← Back to Workbook" button in the yellow banner at the top of the page.

Q: How do I add more team members to my review?

Go to Pre-Review → Assign Review Team, or edit the review from the Admin panel.

Technical

Q: Does this work on mobile?

Yes — the layout is responsive. However, data entry is much easier on a laptop or desktop with a full keyboard.

Q: Does data sync between devices?

Yes — once you've entered the team code, all data syncs automatically through a shared cloud store. Changes appear on other devices within seconds. If you don't see the latest data, refresh the page (Ctrl+Shift+R or pull down on mobile).

Q: Which browsers are supported?

Chrome (recommended), Microsoft Edge, and Firefox. Internet Explorer is not supported.
