Clock Ring Analysis

Upload a TACS clock ring CSV export to compare actual Begin Tour (BT) times against the scheduled start times. Deviations of ±15 minutes or more are flagged. Note: File is not saved.

💡 Recommendation: Pull up to 8 weeks of clock ring data for the most complete analysis. Any number of weeks can be used, but 8 weeks maximum is recommended for accuracy. Employees found in the clock ring data but not on the WebCOINS roster will be flagged — you will need to manually validate whether they are Function 4 employees working in the office.
